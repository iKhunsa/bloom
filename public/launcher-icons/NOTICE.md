# Launcher icons

macOS-style app icons for Bloom's launcher.

- Most files come from the **WhiteSur icon theme** by Vinceliuice
  (https://github.com/vinceliuice/WhiteSur-icon-theme), `src/apps/scalable`,
  licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (same as Bloom).
- The icons listed in `src/launcher/iconIndex.ts` as `BLOOM_IDS` were drawn for Bloom in the same
  style. Brand logos in them come from Simple Icons (CC0, https://simpleicons.org) on a plate in
  the brand's colors; a few use the app's own icon clipped to the same shape.
- App names and logos belong to their owners.
